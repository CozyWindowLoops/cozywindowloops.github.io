Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy explains how Lantern Hours Uploader (“the app”) handles information. The app is a private tool operated by the owner of the Lantern Hours YouTube channel, and is used only to manage that channel.

Use of YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services to upload videos and update video details on the Lantern Hours channel. By using the app, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google’s handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Information the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in, the app receives an access token from Google that allows it to upload videos and set titles, descriptions and thumbnails on the owner’s channel. The app does not access, collect or store information about any other person, and it does not read viewers’ data, comments or messages.

How information is stored and shared

The access token is stored privately on the channel owner’s own computer or automation account, and is used only to upload the channel’s videos. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page. Once revoked, the app can no longer access the channel, and the stored token stops working.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
